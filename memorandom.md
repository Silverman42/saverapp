INTERNAL MEMORANDUM
DATE: 17 September 2026
SUBJECT: Security incidents of April 2026 and 15 September 2026 - recovery efforts and allocation of unrecovered loss

1. Purpose
   This memorandum records Management’s official position on two related security incidents that resulted in financial loss to
   the Company, the recovery actions now underway, and the decision regarding any loss that remains unrecovered after those
   efforts are exhausted.
2. Background
   In April 2026, Hizo suffered a security incident in which unauthorized parties obtained and used production credentials. The
   Company lost over One Hundred and Nineteen Million Naira (₦119,000,000). Following that incident, API keys were rotated
   and recovery efforts were commenced and have continued.
   On 15 September 2026, a further attack occurred. Approximately Eleven Million Five Hundred and Forty-Six Thousand One
   Hundred and Eighty-Four Naira (₦11,546,184) was lost. Investigation established that this second incident was possible
   because compromised credentials from the April incident had been left active in production and had not been deactivated or
   removed, contrary to the controls that should have been completed after the first incident.
3. Recovery
   Management has initiated a fresh recovery programme in respect of the September incident, in addition to the continuing
   efforts relating to April. Engagements with receiving banks and other counterparties are in progress. There has been some
   constructive response from certain banks to which funds were sent. The Company will pursue all reasonable legal, operational,
   and banking channels to recover as much of the diverted funds as possible.
   Recovery is not guaranteed in full. The Company will keep the relevant employees informed, on a need-to-know basis, of
   material developments in the recovery process.
4. Management decision
   Management has determined that the September incident was enabled by negligence: compromised keys known to have been
   exposed in April were permitted to remain in production.
   Accordingly, while the Company will continue recovery efforts in good faith, any financial loss that remains unrecovered after
   all reasonable recovery efforts have been concluded shall be borne by the team members whose negligence caused or
   permitted the lapse. The unrecovered amount attributable to the September incident will be the primary subject of this
   allocation. Any residual position relating to the April incident will be addressed separately if and when recovery on that matter
   is closed.
   The precise amount, the method and timing of any recovery from the responsible employees (including salary offset, repayment
   plan, or other lawful mechanism) will be confirmed in writing once recovery efforts have been substantially concluded or
   Management determines that further recovery is no longer reasonably available. Nothing in this memorandum constitutes a final
   quantification or a deduction from wages until that subsequent written notice is issued and any applicable legal and contractual
   requirements have been observed.
5. Expectations pending conclusion of recovery
   • Give full and prompt cooperation to all internal, banking, and law-enforcement recovery and investigation steps.
   • Do not discuss the incidents, amounts, counterparties, or this memorandum with anyone other than Management,
   designated investigators, or the Company’s legal advisers.
   • Preserve all relevant records, credentials inventories, access logs, and correspondence. Do not delete, alter, or withhold
   material.
   • Complete immediately any outstanding key rotation, secret revocation, and production-access hygiene that remains
   incomplete.
6. Reservation of rights
   This memorandum is an official notice of Management’s decision in principle. It does not waive any right of the Company to take
   further disciplinary, contractual, or legal action arising from either incident, including where recovery is incomplete or where
   additional facts come to light. It is also without prejudice to any rights the Company may have against third parties.
7. Acknowledgement
   Receiving this memo means you are amongst those to be held responsible and accountable for the incident. Please confirm in
   writing, within 24 hours of receipt, that you have read and understood this memorandum. Direct any questions on process to
   Management only.
